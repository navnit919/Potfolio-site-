AI-Based Osteoarthritis Risk Marker Screening System

// 03

An AI-based system engineered for early screening of Osteoarthritis (OA) risk markers in the North Eastern Region (NER). Designed for accessible, patient-friendly screening across remote clinics, healthcare centres, and rural camps where diagnostic infrastructure is limited.

Input: Symptoms + Gait + Sensors + X-Ray ➔ ▪ AI Analysis ➔ ➔ Output: Screening Assessment + Risk Markers + Key Findings
4-Tier Multimodal Solution & Key Features
  • 1. Clinical Questionnaire: Captures joint pain, stiffness, and mobility symptoms through a structured digital intake.
  • 2. Gait & Sensors: Camera with MediaPipe Pose, wearable ESP32 + 3× MPU6050 IMU, and FSR pressure insoles analyze bilateral joint movement, posture, and dynamic weight distribution.
  • 3. Knee X-Ray AI: Deep learning model (DenseNet-121) analyzes knee radiographs for OA-related features including Joint Space Narrowing (JSN) and Kellgren-Lawrence (KL Grades 0·4) with 93.6% confidence.
  • 4. AI + Unified Dashboard: An XGBoost classifier fuses all modalities to generate a composite screening assessment, risk marker breakdown, and multi-axis radar score.
  • Multilingual Localization: Fully translated into Assamese, Bengali, Hindi & English to empower diverse regional healthcare workers and patients.
  • 100% Offline Capable: Fully operational without internet connectivity, making it ideal for rural outreach camps and remote primary health centers.
  • Clinical Scope & Target Users: Serves patients, doctors, physiotherapists, and healthcare workers. Note: The system supports early screening and does not replace professional medical diagnosis.

MineSafe-X · Making Mining Transport Safer

// 01

Developed by Team Colliders, MineSafe-X is a smart safety and fleet monitoring system engineered to reduce collision risks between heavy haul vehicles operating in busy open-cast mines. Mining areas feature limited visibility, blind spots, uneven terrain, and heavy trucks continuously in motion. MineSafe-X gives drivers better awareness, faster warnings, and a safer working environment.

System Architecture & Key Capabilities
  • Multisensory Fusion: Combines GPS, mmWave radar, and ultrasonic time-of-flight sensors to continuously track vehicle location, speed, direction, acceleration, altitude, and distance from nearby heavy vehicles.
  • V2V & V2I Direct Communication: Direct Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) communication enables trucks to exchange telemetry with each other, while Vehicle-to-Infrastructure (V2I) communication interfaces with the central monitoring system.
  • Predictive Time to Collision (TTC): Intelligently calculates dynamic Time to Collision (TTC) vectors and immediately sounds cab alerts via an interactive mobile dashboard when vehicles get dangerously close.
  • Local Communication via ESP-NOW: Operates entirely without cellular or internet connectivity using low-latency peer-to-peer ESP-NOW radio, guaranteeing uncompromised reliability in remote pits.
  • Central Fleet Monitoring Tower: Works as a centralized fleet monitoring system where a central tower keeps track of multiple trucks and displays an overall live overview of all vehicle movements across the mine.

Traffic Violation Detection System

// 02

An AI-powered smart traffic monitoring system engineered to automatically detect, identify, and record traffic violations from live camera footage (Raspberry Pi camera, Arducam, or laptop camera). Eliminates dependence on manual traffic monitoring with faster, consistent, data-driven law enforcement.

Camera ➔ Video Processing ➔ Vehicle Detection ➔ Violation Detection ➔ Number Plate Detection ➔ EasyOCR ➔ Violation Record ➔ Dashboard ➔ AI Traffic Assistant
Core Detection Pipeline & Features
  • YOLOv8 Violation Detection: Real-time computer vision models trained to identify vehicles and flag infractions: Helmet / No-Helmet Riders, Triple Riding, Red-Light Violations, and Wrong-Side Driving.
  • Vehicle Tracking & Unique ID: Integrated tracking logic assigns a persistent Unique ID to vehicles as they traverse camera frames, preventing duplicate violation counts.
  • Number Plate Detection & EasyOCR: A dedicated number-plate model pinpoints the registration plate, crops the bounding box, and passes it to EasyOCR for high-accuracy character recognition (Detected Plate ➔ Crop ➔ EasyOCR ➔ Vehicle Reg Number).
  • Violation Database & Web Dashboard: Stores violation records containing Unique Vehicle ID, vehicle plate number, violation category, date/time, snapshot frame, camera source, and confidence score. Displayed on a modern web dashboard built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and a Python backend.
  • AI Traffic Assistant (LLM Chatbot): Integrated AI assistant analyses violation logs to answer follow-up queries (e.g. "What were the most common violations this week?"), generate weekly/monthly reports, identify violation patterns, and recommend traffic safety improvements.

F450 Multirotor UAV Platform

// 04

Custom-engineered 450mm wheelbase aerial quadcopter architecture built from discrete structural, electronic, and power components during an intensive 5-day hands-on NIELIT technical bootcamp.

Technical Specifications & Architecture
  • Flight Stabilization: Calibrated KK2.1.5 / APM multirotor flight controller with 3-axis gyro and 3-axis accelerometer proportional-integral-derivative (PID) loop tuning for rock-solid hovering.
  • Propulsion Array: 4x 1000KV brushless DC outrunner motors paired with 30A SimonK Electronic Speed Controllers (ESCs) and 1045 composite balance propellers.
  • Power Architecture: High-discharge 3S (11.1V) 2200mAh 25C LiPo battery harnessed through a central power distribution board with dedicated voltage regulators.
  • RF Avionics: 6-channel 2.4GHz FlySky computer transmitter/receiver telemetry with calibrated throttle curves and emergency failsafe cutoff.

Ultrasonic Spatial Radar Bench

// 05

Spatial obstacle detection instrument measuring acoustic time-of-flight wave reflections on an Arduino microcontroller to construct real-time angular radar sweeps with collision boundary warnings.

Technical Specifications & Architecture
  • Acoustic Ranging Physics: Generated microsecond-precise 10µs HIGH trigger pulses on HC-SR04 transducer; measured echo pulse durations to calculate distance via $D = \frac{T \times 0.0343}{2}$ cm.
  • Angular Sweep: Synchronized SG90 servo motor stepping smoothly from 15° to 165° with 30ms dwell times per degree increment.
  • UART Serial Protocol: Transmitted synchronized angle and distance tuples over high-speed COM4 serial at 9600 baud for PC visual scope plotting.
  • Hazard Logic: Configured automatic acoustic alarms when detected obstacles crossed critical safety thresholds (<15cm).
